Six original illustrations were generated using a text-to-image tool for this project. Prompts and the music correction are recorded in [docs/image-prompts.json](docs/image-prompts.json). They are conceptual teaching aids, not measured data, photographs, official product diagrams, exact architectural surveys or historical evidence. Two additional course illustrations for release QA were generated with the host text-to-image model. Prompts and provenance: [Chinese](validation/release-v1.0.0/zh/astronomy-sample-v1/research/image-record.json), [English](validation/release-v1.0.0/en/research/image-prompt.txt). PDFs embed subsets of locally available Times New Roman and FandolFang fonts. Font software is not included in the installable Skill. Fandol fallback is supplied by the user's TeX distribution; proprietary fonts must be supplied by the user's system. PDF generation does not require downloading proprietary fonts.
