# Attribution / 来源与授权

Code, instructions, original lesson text and original diagrams are distributed under MIT. The six introductory courses include a separate source-linked figure for each plan and lesson. Editable figures and reading notes are in each course’s `figure-specs/` folder. Referenced articles remain the property of their respective authors. Omni Learning Anything is an independent project.

The Ming history lesson annotates a detail from *Elegant Gathering in the Apricot Garden*, after Xie Huan, ca. 1437, The Metropolitan Museum of Art, accession 1989.141.3, Purchase, The Dillon Fund Gift, 1989. The museum marks its image as public domain. The color boxes and notes were added for this lesson. [Collection record and image](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/41478) · [The Met’s Open Access policy](https://www.metmuseum.org/about-the-met/policies-and-documents/open-access)

The two illustrations in the v1.0 release-validation archive are AI-generated teaching aids. The contributors offer these images under MIT to the extent of their rights. The archive includes both generation records. [Chinese sample](validation/release-v1.0.0/zh/astronomy-sample-v1/research/image-record.json) · [English sample](validation/release-v1.0.0/en/research/image-prompt.txt)

PDFs embed subsets of locally available Times New Roman and FandolFang fonts. Font software is not included in the installable Skill. Fandol fallback is supplied by the user's TeX distribution; proprietary fonts must be supplied by the user's system. PDF generation does not require downloading proprietary fonts.
