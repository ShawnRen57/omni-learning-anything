# Course catalog sources and verification

The [built-in catalog](../skills/omni-learning-anything/references/course-catalog.json) includes 67 pathways: 53 university courses, 12 AI organization courses and two open references. The original 12 were checked on 2026-11-01. The v3.1.0 and v3.2.0 additions were checked on 2026-11-04.

## What the catalog represents

Course identity, named edition, published order, and original workload statements are grounded in the linked provider, instructor, or official course-platform pages. Unit titles and focus statements are concise, original Chinese paraphrases for navigation. Session grouping, audience suggestions and any preparation explicitly labeled as a recommendation are editorial adaptations. They are approximate learning blueprints, not transcripts, exact teacher quotations, university endorsements, credit-bearing enrollment, or a replacement for the original assignments.

Every unit preserves a contiguous portion of its source sequence. A locator names the original lecture, calendar session, week, or dated syllabus rows; `source_id` points to a source within that course. In a grouped unit spanning several music pages, the locator also lists the related source IDs. Session numbering is never silently treated as video numbering. Exams, guest sessions and reviews remain in their published positions where the source lists them.

`syllabus_and_sessions` records an inspected schedule with source materials for courses, or a table of contents and readable sections for references. It does not mean every recording, reading or assignment was reviewed. `syllabus_only` means the published outline or activity order was inspected. Access to the underlying teaching material was limited or remains unverified. `checked_at` is the source-check date, not a guarantee that a live URL will remain unchanged.

## Reuse and maintenance

The repository stores only short original descriptions, factual identity metadata, locators and links. It does not copy lecture notes, transcripts, full syllabi, solutions, images or recordings. Public access to a source does not establish permission to redistribute third-party material inside it. External platform deadlines, certificates, software environments and present-day institutional facts should be rechecked before relying on them.

When updating a course, verify identity and edition first, then compare the full ordered schedule. Preserve the stable course ID; change the edition and evidence fields explicitly when adopting a different offering. Do not combine a newer syllabus with an older lecture order without documenting the difference. Do not fill a missing source with model recollection.
